# Copperleaf Pika: a PNG animal

Source: source-assets/copperleaf-pika.png

Create a zoo animal based on Thomson’s Gazelle. Draw walk → Blank → Import external art. Select the PNG, One picture, height 38, Repeat for all five directions. This repeats one viewpoint and is a prototype, not five newly invented angles.

Save compiles the game art. For this prototype, Link other poses reuses that art for the other adult states; wait for it to finish. Add its own food, habitat and group values in Needs and the Inspector. Pictures → Use an animation frame makes its icon, list picture and plaque.

## Choose New mod, enter Wildlife Import Tutorial, and use Empty: build it in the studio.

![Copperleaf Pika: a PNG animal](../../renderer/help/media/03f6a7750bb5e5af61.webp)

## Make something → New animal. Choose Zoo animal; this selects its care system.

![Copperleaf Pika: a PNG animal](../../renderer/help/media/e4e859ffb925a154ba.webp)

## Name the species Copperleaf Pika and choose Thomson’s Gazelle as its starting behavior.

![Copperleaf Pika: a PNG animal](../../renderer/help/media/433fd0340ae6641943.webp)

## Choose Draw on walk, then Blank. The direction ring shows the five views that need frames.

![Copperleaf Pika: a PNG animal](../../renderer/help/media/1e6ba5ee1edc650a7c.webp)

## Choose One picture, fit visible art to 38 pixels, and Repeat for all five directions for this simple prototype. Repetition does not invent new viewpoints.

![Copperleaf Pika: a PNG animal](../../renderer/help/media/5a65194403f7b60522.webp)

## Check the visible feet against the red ground point. The importer ignores nearly transparent edge pixels when fitting the art.

![Copperleaf Pika: a PNG animal](../../renderer/help/media/0873493115ba25690e.webp)

## Use Link other poses to reuse the imported art for the prototype’s adult behavior slots. This shares frames; it is not bespoke eating or sleeping animation.

![Copperleaf Pika: a PNG animal](../../renderer/help/media/00e0922751d32415e8.webp)

## Use Needs for food and preferred ground; the Inspector sets adoption price, group size, space and hunger rate. Values save into the mod as they change.

![Copperleaf Pika: a PNG animal](../../renderer/help/media/ddf5721518fccad543.webp)
