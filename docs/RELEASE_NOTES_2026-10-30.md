Everything since v2026.10.20.

# What's new

## Marine shows

- **Show tanks:** Connect an animal's home tank to a show tank, add show equipment and grandstands, then choose the tricks for its performance.
- **Show schedules:** Set how often a show runs, choose continuous performances, or use Never to stop scheduled shows.
- **Marine specialists:** An available specialist is assigned automatically when a show is ready to start.
- **Show animals:** Eligible adults travel to the show through the connecting gate, perform their tricks and return to their home tank afterwards.
- **Shared show tanks:** Several home tanks can connect to one show tank so animals from different exhibits can use it.
- **Trick selection:** The show menu checks whether a trick's research, equipment and water-depth requirements are met.
- **Show species:** Scheduled programs support orcas, dolphins, great white sharks, jellyfish, sea lions and sea otters.
- **Grandstands:** Guests choose a show and sit in individual seats. Each stand links to the show tank it faces.
- **Show admission:** Changing a show's ticket price updates the grandstands linked to that show.

## Saved games

- **Original save import:** Load saved zoos from the original Zoo Tycoon, including more of their animals, guests, workers, exhibits and zoo-management information.
- **Shared zoo files:** The default Save Game option is now Shared zoo (.zoo). One file keeps the full ZT1-Engine zoo and a compatible version that the original game can load.
- **Engine-only saves:** The save dialog also offers Engine-only save (.zt1save) when you only need to continue in ZT1-Engine.
- **Current zoo export:** Shared saves include the zoo as it is now, including compatible animals and objects added after loading an original save.
- **Custom content:** Custom animals, buildings, staff, scenery, filters, food and elevated paths stay in the engine's part of a shared save. The original loads the supported stock content instead.
- **Custom paths and fences:** Shared saves provide stock equivalents for supported custom paths and boundaries when opened in the original game.
- **Animal records:** Original imports restore more information about animal identities, age, young animals, eggs, crates, health and needs.
- **Guest records:** Original imports restore names, appearance, inventory, favourite species, arrival information, needs and thoughts about animals and exhibits.
- **Staff records:** Original imports restore worker appearance, duties and supported exhibit assignments.
- **Research and zoo management:** Imports restore research funding and progress, enabled research categories, awards and marketing settings.
- **Finance history:** More of the original zoo's monthly finances, running totals, cash, date and admission settings are restored.
- **Saved show programs:** Original trick lists, animal species, frequency and admission settings load into the engine. Compatible programs created in the engine can also be saved for the original.
- **Save formats:** The dialog changes the file extension to match the selected format. Keep a separate engine copy before resaving in the original, because the original removes engine-only content from its copy.

## Animals and habitats

- **Animal awareness:** Animals account for nearby animals while moving around their exhibit.
- **Large marine animals:** Swimming and turning take the animal's body size into account near tank walls.
- **Marine plants and rocks:** Habitat ratings use the individual species' preferences instead of treating every marine decoration as suitable.
- **Water requirements:** Marine habitat checks account for water depth, cleanliness, salinity and whether the animal needs land.
- **Mixed habitats:** Animals such as walruses can move between land and water within their enclosure.
- **Dinosaur behaviour:** Expanded handling for eggs, hatching, feeding, rampaging and capture by the Dinosaur Response Team.
- **Animal animations:** Improved handling for multi-step actions, turning towards a target, finishing an animation and catching prey.

## Mods in the game

- **Downloaded mods:** Downloads and imports start disabled. Choose which mods to enable after installing them.
- **In-game mod switches:** Main-menu Enable and Game Options use the same selection. Optional mods can be switched during play.
- **Required mods:** A mod supplying the current map or placed custom content stays enabled while that content is needed. The menu explains the reason.
- **Mod pages:** The in-game list uses separate left/right arrow buttons and a Page 1 of N counter.
- **Mod pictures:** The download menu shows cover images before installation. Lab uploads prepare a cover from the mod's About picture.
- **Download sizes:** Small files show their size in KB instead of rounding down to 0 MB.
- **Custom freeform maps:** Maps supplied by enabled mods appear in map selection with their own picture and description. The custommaps option controls this feature.
- **Custom names:** Mod animals, buildings and staff display their own names in the relevant buy lists and information windows.

## ZT1 Lab mod studio

- **Mods workspace:** Browse a mod's contents by kind, open its artwork and edit its properties in the Inspector. The studio also lists missing parts that need attention.
- **New animals:** Choose Zoo, Marine or Dinosaur, give the animal a name and select a starting species whose behaviour it will use.
- **Existing animals:** Create a separate species from an existing animal, then change its appearance, needs, food and behaviour settings.
- **Animal needs:** Set its carer, food, preferred ground, plants, rocks, shelters and compatible animals, or copy the needs of another species.
- **Marine settings:** Edit depth requirements, swimming height, wall clearance, salinity and water-cleanliness preferences.
- **Dinosaur settings:** Edit egg and hatching properties, egg eating, rampaging and fence-breaking behaviour.
- **Animal pictures:** Create or edit the buy-panel icon, list picture and plaque. A drawn animation frame can be used as the starting picture.
- **Item builders:** Create new items or customise existing buildings, fences, staff, tank filters, plants, rocks, shelters, enrichment and zoo entrances.
- **Staff and guests:** Custom staff use an existing job's duties. Guest artwork can replace one of the four existing guest appearances.
- **Tank filters:** The Inspector exposes cleaning, wear, servicing and upkeep settings for custom filters.
- **Paths:** Create custom path artwork for all twenty connection shapes.
- **Ground textures:** Replace the appearance of an existing ground type while keeping that terrain's gameplay properties.
- **Animal food:** Choose custom food in an animal's Needs page. Its usual keeper or specialist supplies that food.
- **Dinosaur egg artwork:** Edit the egg, hatching sequence and broken shell separately from the adult animal.
- **Remake, Stencil and Blank:** Start artwork from an existing picture, trace an outline, or draw from an empty canvas.
- **Independent artwork:** Own copy separates artwork that was linked to another item so it can be edited independently.
- **Animation editor:** Edit layers and frames, use a faded view of neighbouring frames as a guide, and preview the animal from different directions.
- **Artwork tools:** Resize and reshape artwork, recolour selected shades, paint within a stencil, add stripes or fade a pattern across the body.
- **Editor controls:** Zoom with Ctrl + scroll or the zoom bar. The stencil browser previews animals, people, buildings, plants and fences before selection.
- **Building artwork:** Edit idle and in-use animations together with the building's static background and default colours.
- **PNG imports:** Import a single picture, frame strip or directional sprite sheet, fit it to the game's size and preview it before saving.
- **OBJ imports:** Render a model into game directions. Several supplied poses can be turned into an animation sequence; material and texture files provide its colours.
- **Animation links:** Reuse an existing pose for selected animal actions while building up a new animal's artwork.
- **Game-file editing:** Copy an existing game definition into a mod to change values such as an animal's price or a building's capacity. Files already inside a mod can be edited directly.
- **Mod pictures:** Use your own image, a drawn frame or a Test Zoo picture for the mod's cover.
- **Starter kit:** A starter mod provides examples of the different kinds of content a mod can contain.

## Custom maps, theme packs and Help

- **Map editor:** Build terrain, water, hills, paths and scenery in the Test Zoo, then save the design into a mod.
- **Blank maps:** Start at Small, Medium or Large size, or choose your own dimensions. Set the zoo boundary and place its entrance.
- **Terrain view:** Inspect the map from above while working on its ground layout.
- **Theme packs:** Select several compatible mods and publish them as one named download with a picture and description.
- **Pack conflicts:** Conflicting files are reported before creating a pack. The original individual mods remain available for editing.
- **Tutorial mods:** Wildlife Import Tutorial, Park Workshop Tutorial and Moonstone Habitat Kit are available separately or together as Moonstone Zoo Theme.
- **Help tab:** A tab beside Mods contains 21 searchable guides, illustrated lessons and nine videos. Press F1 to open it.
- **Help topics:** Guides cover the Lab workspaces, animal and object imports, habitats, maps, testing, theme packs and publishing or downloading mods.
- **Offline Help:** Included guides and tutorial media can be viewed without an internet connection. Older recordings are labelled for their earlier interface.
- **Lab mod tests:** Test Zoo and Play with it use temporary mod selections, keeping an experiment separate from the mods enabled for normal play.

## Game windows

- **Side-by-side windows:** Zoo Status, animal, guest, exhibit and staff lists, information windows and a buy panel can stay open together. New windows use nearby clear space. The smartstack option controls this.
- **Window dragging:** Move windows by their title bars, including Game Options, and remember their positions between sessions. The dragwindows option controls this.
- **Window locks:** A gold lock beside the close button prevents accidental dragging. Each window remembers its lock setting.
- **Window positions:** Game Options > Controls > Window Positions > Reset restores window positions while keeping their locks.
- **Information lists:** The optional infocloseslists setting closes a source list when its information window opens.
- **Main menu:** The version number is displayed in the corner.

# Bug fixes

## Marine shows and staff

- **Automatic shows:** Ready shows recruit a marine specialist without the player having to assign one manually.
- **Dolphin performers:** Both eligible adult dolphins can join the same show instead of only one being selected.
- **Show equipment:** Required toys and equipment placed in the show tank are recognised when choosing tricks.
- **Home-tank animations:** Waving, water-spitting and other performance animations no longer play randomly in an animal's normal exhibit.
- **Dolphin flips:** The renderer keeps the full animal visible while it is above the water.
- **Marine specialist platforms:** Corrected the worker's standing height on the platform.
- **Specialist ladders:** Corrected the direction of climbing animations when going up or down.
- **Grandstand spectators:** Corrected guest height and individual seating positions.
- **Interrupted shows:** Animals keep their home assignment and return through the connecting gate when a show is interrupted.
- **Stranded performers:** Animals left in a show tank recover their home when it can be identified. An unavailable route leaves them in a transport capsule for relocation.
- **Show-tank feeding:** Specialists feed animals in their home tanks. Show tanks clear ordinary food piles while keeping trick rewards available.
- **Blocked platforms:** Connecting another tank or placing a building can move the platform to a clear bank. The fence gate tool can also relocate it.
- **Platform changes:** Moving a platform during a performance cancels and resets that show safely.
- **Show admission:** Updating one show's price updates its linked stands rather than requiring each stand to be edited separately.
- **Show frequency:** The Never setting is available and keeps its value after saving and loading.

## Tanks, placement and demolition

- **Large swimmers:** Corrected animals drawing through tank walls while turning, including views near platforms and filters.
- **Tank gates:** Gates open and close with their animations and draw in the correct order against passing animals.
- **Underwater placement:** Animal, plant and rock previews use the tank floor instead of appearing on the water surface before placement.
- **Show-toy placement:** Previews use the appropriate water height, and toys draw correctly near the specialist platform.
- **Marine plants:** Underwater plants require a filled tank instead of being accepted on ordinary land.
- **Demolition targets:** The highlighted object matches the one selected for removal, making toys, plants, equipment and nearby walls easier to distinguish.
- **Tank floors:** Original-save imports preserve the floor's terrain. Removing a tank uses its saved ground or the surrounding ground when no earlier ground record exists.
- **Tank filter animations:** Running filters animate and hum. Worn and broken filters use their appropriate appearance and sounds; filters beside empty tanks switch off.
- **Filter maintenance:** Workers service filters without restarting their existing wear countdown.
- **Custom filters:** Separate filter definitions retain their own artwork, footprint, cleaning rate, wear, upkeep, servicing and refund values.

## Saving and loading

- **Failed loads:** The incoming zoo is checked before replacing the open zoo. A failed load preserves the current session.
- **Missing mod content:** Load errors name a missing custom map or definition so the required mod can be enabled.
- **Startup loading:** Save restoration waits until catalogue and research loading is ready before using that information.
- **Save-file validation:** Damaged or truncated shared-save data is rejected instead of silently dropping its engine content.
- **Repeated saves:** Saving again no longer puts another copy of the previous save inside the file.
- **Save-file writing:** A temporary file replaces the destination only after writing finishes.
- **Converted saves:** A converted zoo can be reopened without the original file still being at its previous location.
- **Animal restoration:** Preserved young animals, eggs, crates, lifetime information, health and need timers across supported imports and reloads.
- **Guest restoration:** Preserved more guest appearance, inventory, favourite-animal and recent-visit information. Reordering animal definitions does not change a saved favourite.
- **Staff restoration:** Preserved supported worker appearance, duties and exhibit assignments.
- **Research and finances:** Corrected restoration of research settings, financial history, income and expense signs, awards and marketing values.
- **Tank equipment saves:** Filters remain working equipment after conversion, with saved condition and supported costs. Engine saves retain service countdowns and the selected filter type.
- **Original species references:** Mods replacing stock animals keep the correct stock animal identity when saving for the original game.
- **Custom show programs:** Tricks requiring engine-only definitions stay with the engine content instead of producing an invalid original program.
- **Original entrances and boundaries:** Stock entrance pieces survive export, custom tank walls use a compatible stock wall, and older custom-map objects receive valid records.
- **Original tank contents:** Submerged generic dung no longer prevents the original game from filling a tank. The engine still keeps that dung in its own saved state.
- **Camera restoration:** Saved position, zoom and all four rotations restore correctly, including supported original-game views.
- **Zoo changes:** Corrected loading another zoo from inside a game and returning to the main menu. Looping zoo and building sounds stop when switching zoos.
- **Older food records:** Older converted saves using generic food references are mapped to the appropriate food.

## Buildings, dinosaurs and guests

- **DRT base:** Corrected the Dinosaur Response Team building's grid alignment and its position when rotating the map.
- **DRT helicopter:** Corrected helicopter animations and dinosaur capture duties so the response team handles dinosaur capture.
- **Shops and attractions:** Buildings use their available idle, customer-use and service animations.
- **Building signs and animated parts:** Restaurants, gift shops, DRT buildings and other structures keep their parts aligned in each camera direction.
- **Building entrances:** Guests enter through the door on the correct rotated side. A path touching another wall no longer counts as door access.
- **Custom buildings:** Mod-defined animation paths work during placement, use and after loading a saved map.
- **Custom staff:** Workers listed in enabled mod registries can be hired and carry out their inherited duties.
- **Custom names:** Buildings and hired staff show the names supplied by their mods.
- **Dinosaur feeding:** Eating poses, including head-down feeding, consume food correctly.
- **Rampaging dinosaurs:** Dinosaurs approach a suitable building before damaging it.
- **Egg hatching:** Corrected the handling of healthy hatches and failed eggs.
- **Guest tours:** Guests leave tours when they need a building or must leave. Guides clear their followers when a tour finishes or the guide is fired.
- **Guest thoughts:** Time-in-park thoughts use the original game's thresholds.
- **Custom entrances:** Mod entrances provide the zoo's guest entry path.
- **Custom bins:** Bins can use their own art and participate in rubbish collection.

## Interface and camera

- **Game Options:** Opening the menu pauses the zoo. Closing it resumes a running zoo and leaves a manually paused zoo paused.
- **Mod list:** Names, required-content explanations, page controls and help text no longer run into each other.
- **Tundra (Small):** The full entrance is recognised as part of the zoo boundary, restoring construction inside the zoo.
- **Placement rotation:** Turning a held object with Q or E no longer makes it disappear until the mouse moves.
- **Starting cash:** Freeform remembers the selected amount, and its difficulty label follows that amount. Scenarios use their own starting funds.
- **Date display:** The date remains visible while the zoo is paused.
- **Window dragging:** Zoo Status, recommendations, plaques, messages, Help and other movable windows drag by their titles correctly.
- **Staff Information:** The panel opens on its Status page, and pictures in the Staff List display at their intended size.
- **Button help:** Help appears after a click as well as after moving the pointer.
- **Game Options controls:** The sidebar button closes the window again, and the Game page's buttons are positioned correctly.
- **Camera drawing:** Reduced work for terrain and objects outside the camera view, including while placing animals, shops, scenery and paths.
- **Tank drawing:** Tank geometry is reused during each frame instead of recalculating the same work repeatedly.
- **Show crowds:** Readiness and attendance decisions avoid unnecessary repeated work each frame.

## ZT1 Lab and downloads

- **Lab pointer controls:** Closing a native game dialog or stopping Play no longer leaves the Lab's window and title-bar controls unclickable.
- **Embedded Play:** The game's position follows Lab resizing, minimising and restoring.
- **Lab replies:** Startup and command responses are read completely, preventing missed readiness messages and incomplete results.
- **Test Zoo loading:** Map loads wait for their own completion reply. Choosing a map cancels a delayed startup rebuild that could replace it.
- **Temporary mod selections:** Ending a mod test restores the normal selection. A newly installed Lab does not carry developer test selections.
- **Recorded setups:** The Test Zoo setup is kept before rebuilding or restarting after a mod change.
- **Map editing:** Reopened blank maps can be saved again, saved ground drawings restore correctly, and imported packs select the correct installed folder.
- **Building artwork editor:** Static backgrounds and default colours are kept with animated parts. Sources with diagonal artwork open on a direction containing a picture.
- **Portable Game Build:** The ZIP includes the mod-download client and creates its own key on first startup, preserving an existing key.
- **Lab installer:** Included the process-output helper required by the updated Play controls.
- **CodeRook publishing:** Source updates preserve protected build files, and a failed source upload stops before downloads can be attached to the wrong version.
